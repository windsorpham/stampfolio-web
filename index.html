Stampfolio

Turn your travel memories into beautiful digital stamps.

Stampfolio is the ultimate app for travelers and photography enthusiasts. Capture your moments, apply beautiful vintage and modern stamp templates, and collect them in your personalized digital scrapbook.

Features include:

  • Custom stamp borders (Classic, Nippon, Postal, Square)
  • Location-based stamp unlocking
  • Digital Scrapbook with customizable text and washi tapes
  • iCloud Sync to keep your collections safe
